Nine years of 2 AM tickets.

Since 2017 we have hosted QuickBooks, Sage and tax software for US accounting firms. Our team works in Kathmandu, so our customers' nights have always been our days.

Most of what we fixed overnight wasn't hard. It was urgent: a company file that wouldn't open, a printer before a filing deadline, a password at 11 PM.

That turned out to be what we do best. So we're moving out of hosting and putting the whole team on the after-hours desk, for MSPs and hosting providers, inside their tools and under their name.
